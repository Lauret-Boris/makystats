ARDL

Autoregressive distributed lag models: bounds test, UECM, restricted ECM, long-run coefficients, and level forecasts.

from makystats import ardl, select_ardl_order

res = ardl(y, x=x, lags=2, order=2, case=3).fit()
print(res.summary())
print(res.bounds)            # Pesaran / Narayan F and t
print(res.long_run_table)

# Restricted ECM then forecast
ecm = res.ecm()
fc = res.forecast(steps=6)

# Diagnostics
print(res.diagnostic_summary())

# Order selection (homogeneous or per-variable maxorder)
tab = select_ardl_order(y, x=x, maxlag=4, maxorder=2, case=3, ic="bic")

Cases (deterministic)

caseMeaning
1No constant, no trend
2Restricted constant
3Unrestricted constant
4Restricted trend
5Unrestricted constant and trend

Extras

  • fixed= regressors outside the cointegrating relation
  • diagnostic_summary()
  • CUSUM / CUSUMSQ stability (user-chosen significance levels)
  • Index-preserving residuals and fitted values
