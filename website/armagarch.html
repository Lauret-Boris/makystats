ARMA–GARCH

Joint maximum-likelihood estimation of the mean and conditional variance. Supports seasonal mean terms and the same lag conventions as arma.

Quick start

from makystats import arma_garch, select_order

res = arma_garch(y, mean="ARMA", ar=1, ma=1, d=0,
                 r=1, s=1, trend="c", dist="normal").fit()
print(res.summary())

# With seasonal mean
res = arma_garch(y, mean="ARMA", ar=0, ma=1, d=1,
                 seasonal_order=(1, 1, 1, 12),
                 r=1, s=1, trend="c").fit()

# Information criteria grid
tab = select_order(y, max_ar=2, max_ma=2, max_r=1, max_s=1,
                   d=0, trend="c", ic="bic")

Innovations

dist: normal, studentst, ged, skewt (where implemented).

Post-estimation

  • Index-aligned residuals, conditional volatility, fitted mean
  • Forecasts of mean and variance
  • Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall helpers
  • select_order supports seasonal_order for comparable IC grids
